# Global Vehicle Sales Analysis | Tableau Business Intelligence Dashboard

**Cross-country vehicle market analysis combining sales, economic, and demographic data to uncover patterns through interactive visualization.**

[**Explore the Interactive Tableau Dashboard**](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/ahmet.gen./viz/AhmetGenStat112Project/DASHBOARD)

## Project Overview

This project brings together global vehicle sales data and economic indicators in an interactive Tableau dashboard. Rather than examining sales figures in isolation, the analysis places vehicle market activity in a broader economic and demographic context to support more meaningful comparisons across countries.

The workflow covers **data preparation, multi-source integration, feature engineering, exploratory analysis, and dashboard development**—from organizing raw inputs in Excel to translating relationships among market indicators into accessible visualizations.

## What I Built

- **Integrated multiple datasets:** Prepared and standardized global vehicle sales and economic data in **Excel**, then used **Tableau joins** to connect the sources for cross-country analysis.
- **Engineered comparison metrics:** Created **Tableau calculated fields**, including **GDP per capita** and **Price-to-MSRP Ratio**, to enrich the analysis beyond raw sales and pricing figures.
- **Explored market relationships:** Examined how sales indicators relate to economic and demographic variables, using visual comparisons to investigate variation among countries.
- **Developed an interactive dashboard:** Combined **scatter plots, bar charts, and bubble visualizations** to make complex, multi-variable comparisons easier to explore and interpret.
- **Communicated findings visually:** Organized the analysis around clear, accessible data visualizations that support exploratory, data-driven interpretation of global vehicle market trends.

## Analytical Focus

The dashboard is designed to investigate questions such as:

1. How do vehicle sales indicators vary across countries?
2. What relationships can be explored between vehicle market activity and economic conditions?
3. How can metrics such as GDP per capita and Price-to-MSRP Ratio improve cross-country comparisons?
4. Which patterns become more visible when sales, demographic, and economic indicators are examined together?

These are **exploratory questions**, not claims of causal relationships.
